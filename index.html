Meet the Characters

"Welcome to Formula Racing Camp"

Where Every Victory Carries Their Voice

Nithilan

Team: Formula Racing Camp

Status: 5x Best F1 Coach Award Winner

Signature: Turning the tables for his students when defeat looks certain ☮️

Partner: Pro-Comp-So-Rotatus 5.0 (robot computer)

"Believe in yourself & the track, & you'll soon find the finish line."

A coach & computer specialist. He has a Pro AI robo-computer, the Pro-Comp-So-Rotatus 5.0 (PCSR 5)

The PCSR 5 does very fast computer simulations with built-in mini-GPUs to calculate the exact way the race should be raced by his students. That often saves the game in the last few minutes.

Nithilan is the calm mind behind the speed. With a composed attitude essential for coaching, he guides racers with patience, clarity, and confidence. He encourages his students to trust their skills, sharpen their focus, and push themselves to be better with every race.

As the chief strategist of Formula Racing Camp, Nithilan ensures that every F1 car his students race in is equipped with advanced onboard intelligence. Each vehicle contains a built-in performance computer known as the Pro-Comp-So-Vehiculus (PCSV)—a system designed to monitor speed, balance, energy output, tyre performance, and racing lines in real time. The screens are on the steering wheel.

For example, Jaskirat's Neon Raptor-Vortex GT-X's PCSVs are in constant communication with the PCSR 5 to give fast instructions as well as audio contact with Nithilan during racing.

Nithilan may not sit in the car, but every victory carries his voice.

Motto: Strategy Wins Races. Intelligence Conquers Speed. 🧠⚡

Nandana

Team: Formula Racing Camp

Status: Racer with Integrity & Cultural Pride 🏆

Name Meaning: Happiness (Sanskrit) 🌟

Signature: Racing with Integrity, Speed & Grace

Partner: Amulya (Peacock)

"Race with grace and integrity to win"

Nandana means happiness in Sanskrit—
and she brings that joy to every race. 🌸

Deeply rooted in Indian culture and history, Nandana races with a connection to the past and a vision for the future. She is confident behind the wheel, ready to face any challenge with grace and determination.

Unlike many who only focus on victory, Nandana races with integrity and sportsmanship. Win or lose, she respects the track, her competitors, and the spirit of racing itself. Against all odds, she pushes forward—not just for herself, but for the values she holds dear.

Beyond racing, Nandana's interests flourish in the arts—dancing, singing, and writing. But on race day, it's where all her passions converge into a flourish of speed and elegance on the track. 💃🎶✍️

By her side is Amulya—
a peacock representing the pride and beauty of India. 🦚🇮🇳

Amulya gives Nandana courage when the race gets tough. With feathers that shimmer in the sun and eyes that see every turn ahead, Amulya is the best companion a racer could ask for. Together, they embody tradition meeting speed, heritage meeting innovation.

She doesn't just race to win—
she races to honor the journey. 🌟🌺

With every lap, Nandana explores the past while blazing toward the future.
With every turn, she shows that integrity and speed can coexist.
With every race, she proves that happiness is the greatest fuel of all. 🌟⚡

Igneous

Role: Thunder Beast of F1 Camp (kinda like a mascot) 🔥⚡

Core Power: Living Lightning & Magma 😎

Bonded Driver: Jaskirat

Igneous is not a pet.
He is power given form. 💪

Igneous is no ordinary iguana (well obviously he is, but there's more) - he is a rare quantum reptile capable of shape shifting at will. By manipulating his cellular structure, he can transform into different "living" objects, creatures or even energy-based forms to adapt to any situation.

Forged from lightning and motion, Igneous feeds on speed. His scales glow with stored energy, his eyes burn electric blue, and every spark he releases surges straight into the machine. When he climbs onto the nose of the car, the engine awakens. ⚡🌩️

Igneous feels the track before the wheels touch it. He reacts in milliseconds, charging acceleration, stabilizing balance, and unleashing thunder at the perfect moment. Fire follows the tires. Electricity wraps the car. Rivals feel him before they see him.

Bound to Jaskirat by trust and rhythm, Igneous amplifies precision into dominance. Where others rely on fuel and fear, Igneous runs on pure momentum. 💨😎

On the grid, he watches.
On the launch, he strikes.
On the finish line, his glow fades—victory complete, like always! 🌟🏆🔥

Motto: Born of Lightning & Flame. Feared by Speed. 🔥⚡🦎
